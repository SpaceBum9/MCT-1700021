# Tick vs action

A tick is the clock. An action rides a tick. HOLD is a tick with no action. Execute is not in this game.

Clocks: physics 1, Euclid every 8 physics (16-slot necklace), trip every 24, episode 150.

E(7,16) `x.x.x..x.x.x.x..` — 7 ACTION, 9 HOLD. Gaps 2 2 3 2 2 2 3.

On ACTION: packet, mint, hear, livecode line (`execute: false`). Pit skills **apply**.
On HOLD: wheel still steps. No packet.

`TR-` is a 32-bit nickname. Full FNV-1a-64 is the record. Not collision-resistant. Not a grant to execute.
